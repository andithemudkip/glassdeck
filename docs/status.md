D7 = `cycle[counter mod 6] ⊕ f(D0..D6)` on the 9 cycle-family IDs (`120`, `121`, `129`, `12A`, `12D`, `12E`, `541`, `5A0`, `5B0`). Universal 6-element reference cycle `{0x35,0x5F,0x6A,0x8B,0xBE,0xD4}` (Gray-code in `{0x35,0x6A,0xE1}` XOR basis); the "per-ID offset" originally cataloged was a measurement artifact (we sampled `f` at each ID's dominant payload). `f(D0..D6)` is a 5-bit GF(2)-linear hash given by 5 explicit XOR masks over the 56 payload bits — verifies 1040/1040 distinct payloads with zero residual. `540` and `450` are outside the cycle family: D7 is statically `0x00` across 1027 distinct `540` payloads and 27k `450` frames. Phase 5 novel-payload TX now unblocked modulo a ≤6-frame phase-lock per ID.
  - [`findings/can/engine-state-bits-decay-shape.md`](findings/can/engine-state-bits-decay-shape.md) — `provisional`. None is a fast "engine-running" indicator — use [[signal-rpm]] for that. The two `540` bits are now refined to **"ignition-armed permission"** (transient drops during kill→STOP in the engine-off kill session, per [[2026-06-21-cross-session-payload-diff]]); the three `121` bits remain pure engine-physical-run indicators. Residual question is which sensor sources each bit (needs external mapping or hardware probing).
  - [`findings/can/signal-warmup-index.md`](findings/can/signal-warmup-index.md) — `provisional`. `540` D1 is engine-on-gated and **decreases** monotonically with coolant temp (~0x19 at 26 °C cold start → 0x0E at 92 °C operating temp). Hot restart at 75 °C reads 0x0E immediately with no transient, so it's a stateless thermal lookup, not a time-based cold-start timer. Most likely an ECU warm-up correction (cold-start enrichment factor or fast-idle / idle-air-bypass position) — not the dashboard gauge needle. Was previously catalogued as `signal-coolant-derived` with an inverted reading. Promote on a continuous overnight-cold → fan-cycle warm-up capture plus a physical-quantity ID.
  - [`findings/can/signal-clutch.md`](findings/can/signal-clutch.md) — **new (2026-06-23)**, `confirmed`. Clutch lever at `129` D0 bit 3 (1=pulled, 0=released). The lever switch on this physical bike is mechanically finicky — only trips on full-travel upward pulls past a threshold, which explains the original 2026-06-19 Phase A clutch-only null result (pumps too shallow). Husqvarna-specific — KTM 690 doesn't broadcast clutch at all per [[ktm-can-decoder]].
  - [`findings/can/signal-shift-failed.md`](findings/can/signal-shift-failed.md) — `provisional` (renamed from `signal-shift-lever` after the bit-3 re-attribution). `129` D0 **bit 1** = shift attempt failed to engage target gear (transient — fired ~1.5 s after each of the two failed N→2 attempts in Phase B, never after successful shifts).   - [`findings/can/signal-gear-position.md`](findings/can/signal-gear-position.md) — `confirmed` (promoted 2026-06-23).

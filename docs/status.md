Encoding confirmed (100 % sign-byte consistency across 35,112 engine-on frames in 4 sessions). Engine-on: small wobble within ±2 LSB at idle, climbs through the rear-spin RPM sweep to a **mid-RPM peak of +7 LSB at ~3500 RPM**, drops again at higher RPM; std grows with RPM (0.5 idle → 4 at 5000 RPM).
